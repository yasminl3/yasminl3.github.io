The Capital of Canada

Why Do People Mistake Toronto as the Capital of Canada?

It's often a misconception that Toronto is the Capital City of Canada because Toronto is the largest city in Canada.

Toronto is the capital of the province of Ontario. However, the capital of Canada is Ottawa, which is located on the border between Ontario and Quebec. This location was chosen by Queen Victoria in 1857 due to its strategic and political advantages, including defensibility and a balance between the two provinces. Ottawa's status as the capital is confirmed by historical records and the fact that it is the seat of Canada's federal government.

Reasons why Toronto is considered the Capital city of Canada

  • Toronto's population is 2,731,571 but Ottawa's population is 1,017,449.
  • The area size of Toronto is 664 km², in comparison to Ottawa which is 135 km².
  • Toronto is the country's largest city, the financial capital, the tech hub, and the immigration gateway. Ottawa is the federal capital, smaller, quieter, dominated by the public service, and 1,100 dollars a month cheaper on rent.

  3. The Maple Leafs: Toronto is famous for this relic of a team in hockey history. The Maple Leafs were one of the original six teams that founded the NHL.
